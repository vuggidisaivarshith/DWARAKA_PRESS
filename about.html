About Dwaraka Press

Our Story

Born from a love of books and ideas

Dwaraka Press is an independent book publishing house under the Dwaraka Group / Ventures umbrella. We believe that great books change lives — and we are committed to bringing the best of Indian stories, ideas, and knowledge to readers everywhere.

From picture books for the youngest readers to comprehensive reference works for scholars, Dwaraka Press publishes across four specialised imprints, each with its own voice and vision.

"Stories That Stay. Knowledge That Grows."

Our Four Imprints

Dwaraka Kids

Children's books and colouring books that spark imagination, nurture creativity, and build a love for reading from the earliest age.
